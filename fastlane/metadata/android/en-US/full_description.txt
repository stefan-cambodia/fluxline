FLUXLINE is an original pipe puzzle game in the tradition of the late-1980s arcade puzzlers.

An educational puzzle game for the whole family: it exercises logic, planning ahead and spatial reasoning.

The dispenser hands you the pieces one at a time: place them to build a network before the plasma surges out of the source. Every pipe it flows through scores points; one leak and the round is over.

FOUR MODES
• Campaign: 20 hand-made levels, each built around one idea (crossovers, reservoirs, one-way pipes, a drain to reach…), with stars to earn. A tutorial guides your first steps.
• Classic: the original rules, no rotation. How far can you go?
• Arcade: fast pace, rotate the next piece, three lives.
• Daily challenge: a new board and a new goal every day.

BUILT FOR TOUCH
• Tap a tile to place the piece, or drag it from the dispenser.
• Preview before you let go, generous touch areas, one-handed play.
• Subtle vibration feedback, can be turned off.

PLASMA THAT FEELS ALIVE
Ceramic and brass pipes, glowing plasma that follows every bend, particles and bloom, up to 120 frames per second on supported screens.

RESPECTFUL
• No ads, no in-app purchases.
• No data collected, no Internet connection needed.
• Your game is saved automatically.
• In English, French, Russian, Chinese and Khmer.
